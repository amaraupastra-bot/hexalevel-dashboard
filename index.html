HEXALEVEL — SCENARIO C (3 MEMBERS · 2 WORKING · 1 SKIPPED)
Mathematical Rule: Active members get 23.33% direct pay. The skipped member gets PKR 0.00. The unallocated 23.33% transfers to Company Share (Company gets 53.33%). In Net Profit attribution, surplus is credited ONLY to working members.

📌 Mathematical Mechanics of the Skip Rule:

  • Direct Project Payment: The 2 working members receive 23.33% direct project share each. The non-participating member is marked SKIP and receives PKR 0.00.
  • Company Earnings Transfer: The unallocated third-member share (23.33%) is automatically added to Company Share (Base 30% + 23.33% = 53.33% Total Company Share).
  • Net Profit Attribution: In company net profit distributions, the surplus earned from this project is strictly credited 50/50 between the two active members. The skipped member receives 0 surplus. Consequently, the working members' net profit increases while the skipped member's net profit does not increase.
